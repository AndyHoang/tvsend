# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The full text at that link applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests,
discussions and any other place where the project is represented.

## In short

- Be respectful and patient. People here have different backgrounds and levels of
  experience.
- Criticise ideas and code, not people.
- No harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery, or publishing of other
  people's private information.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour is unacceptable, report it to the project maintainer:

**Contact: ngocdunghoangdl@gmail.com**

Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, commits and
other contributions that break this code, and may block people who repeatedly or
seriously break it, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
